**Description:**
```
Everyone knows Nelson Mandela — prison, peace, the Nobel Prize, ending
apartheid without civil war.

But in his first term as president, Mandela's government dropped the economic
plan voters had queued for in 1994: share the mines, the banks, and the land.
The RDP was replaced by GEAR — budget cuts, privatisation, and fiscal discipline.
Mandela never publicly called it his biggest mistake.

Thirty years on, this video walks through what that choice cost: cadre
deployment, land reform stuck at eleven percent, youth unemployment at six
in ten, and a gap like Sandton and Alexandra — five hundred metres apart.
```
