Film all TH segments in ONE session. Total on-camera time in final video: ~5.5 min.

SESSION ORDER (film in this sequence — not script order):
1. Section 5 analysis (longest TH block — freshest delivery)
2. The July 2021 food sequence + SAHRC follow-through (emotionally centred)
3. Hook declaration ("They say it's corruption. That's not the answer.")
4. Section 1 RDP/GEAR pivot ("They actually had a plan...")
5. Section 2 thesis ("had not spent eighty years learning to run a power company")
6. Sponsor read (last — most rehearsed by then)

SETUP:
- Camera: chest-to-head, slight off-centre
- Background: dark, clean, minimal
- Lighting: key light 45° camera-left, slight fill right, backlight to separate
- Wear: smart casual — no logos, no stripes, dark tones

DELIVERY NOTES — Qaanitah Hunter style:

- **Hook thesis delivery:**
  "They say it's corruption. That's not the answer."
  Say it flat. Not combative. Not triumphant. Like someone correcting a friend
  who means well but has the wrong information.
  Then pause — 1 full second — before continuing.

- **RDP/GEAR reveal:**
  "The ANC secured political power. The corporations secured their property rights."
  Two parallel sentences. Equal weight. Say them as a matched pair.
  Then: "That was the deal." Short. Final. No follow-up.

- **The food sequence (Section 3):**
  This is the video's moral centre. Film it last-but-one so you have emotional
  energy for it.
  "Not phones. Not jewellery. Not televisions."
  Three separate sentences. Three separate beats. Pause between each.
  Then: "Food. Baby formula. Cooking oil. Bread. Rice. Nappies."
  Read it like a list on a warehouse inventory sheet. No drama.
  The drama is in the flatness.
  "Twenty-seven years after the miracle of 1994. People were looting for food."
  Pause before it. Say it once. Don't repeat it. The camera holds. Silence does
  the rest.

- **After the SAHRC quote card:**
  "The government's own finding. After twenty-seven years of democracy."
  Let the quote card do the work. You are simply noting the source.
  Then the line: "That is not a story about corruption." — Say it without
  emphasis. The weight is already there.

- **US comparison (Section 5):**
  Conversational, not lecturing. You're sharing something that surprised you.
  "South Africa is not the only country to try this." — Delivery: matter-of-fact.
  The comparison data card handles the visual evidence. You just set it up.

- **The closing lines:**
  "It just gives inequality a new address." — Deliver it quietly. This is
