## YOUTUBE METADATA (draft)

**Description:**
```
In October 2019, experts warned that male depression, suicide, and stigma
were converging into a crisis. By 2020, loneliness was already epidemic —
and COVID stripped away the pubs, teams, and clubs where men used to connect.

Everyone blamed screens. New research asks whether gaming communities —
digital third places — might actually help isolated men build support.

This video walks through what went wrong, why gaming was feared, what
studies from Texas A&M and South Korea found, and where the limits are:
gaming is not therapy, and it is not a substitute for real-life friendship.
```
