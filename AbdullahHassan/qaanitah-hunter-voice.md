# Qaanitah Hunter Voice — For Abdullah Scripts

> How to borrow **Qaanitah Hunter**'s political-reporting rhythm without copying her persona.  
> Use with [`writing-style-guide.md`](writing-style-guide.md) — Abdullah's rules still win on facts, structure, and AI tells.

**Reference works:** *Balance of Power*, *Eight Days in July* (News24 / NB Publishers).

---

## What to take from Qaanitah

| Qaanitah habit | In your scripts |
|----------------|-----------------|
| **Scene first, thesis second** | Open sections with *where we are in time* + *what people were doing*, then the policy point |
| **Connect the dots out loud** | *"And this is the decision that explains…"*, *"So what happened next?"*, *"That is the part most headlines skip"* |
| **Reporting texture** | *"Mandela would later say…"*, *"The records show…"*, *"If you pull up the numbers…"* — not anonymous essay voice |
| **Human cost stays visible** | Queues, food on the floor, Sandton–Alexandra — policy always tied to a person or place |
| **Urgency without hype** | Day-by-day clarity on scandals (July 2021, Zuma years); no *game changer*, no trailer poetry |
| **Fair, pointed** | Acknowledge Mandela's constraints in one honest beat, then name what the compromise cost |
| **Sharp plain English** | Cut economist filler (*macroeconomy*, *structural transition* as labels); say what happened |

---

## What stays Abdullah (do not drop)

- **International audience** — explain apartheid, ANC, Eskom, tenders, cadre deployment on first use  
- **Oral pivots** — *right?*, *If only*, *That being said… Yeah*, *Let me be straight*  
- **No essay validation** — see [`avoid-ai-sounding-writing.md`](avoid-ai-sounding-writing.md) §4.5b  
- **Speak vs show** — one stat spoken once; callbacks point at charts  
- **TH title line** — *"This is [Title]."*  
- **Policy forks taught out loud** — Qaanitah uses story, not *Option A/B* labels; describe the two paths in full sentences instead

## Sentence patterns (before → after)

| Too 2and20 / essay | More Qaanitah + Abdullah |
|--------------------|---------------------------|
| *Option A: break up white economic power… Option B: keep the structure* | *Mandela could move hard on who owned the economy — and risk a run on the rand on day one. Or he could steady the ship first, cut the deficit, and hope growth reached the poorest over time.* |
| *That is the tension at the centre of his legacy* | *So here is the split in his legacy.* |
| *The bottom layer is… the middle layer is…* | Tell it as a timeline: one decision → what it produced → what scandal added |
| *In today's video we will explore* | *To understand 1994, you need to know what the ANC was selling before a single ballot was cast.* |

## Section openers (template)

1. **Time + place:** *"By 1996, inside the Union Buildings…"*  
2. **Reporter pull:** *"If you pull up the land audit…"*  
3. **Ground truth:** *"On the ground in July 2021…"*  
4. **Honest TH:** *"Let me be straight with you — Mandela is not the villain in this story."*
5. **Policy forks need WHY:** Name the pressures before the choice — inherited economy, market panic, corporate/IMF lobby — then state the bet (*growth first, redistribution later*).
