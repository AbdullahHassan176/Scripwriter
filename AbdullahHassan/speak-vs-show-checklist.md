# Speak vs Show — Editorial Checklist

> How to decide what gets spoken VO/TH vs what lives on a card only.  
> Built from **2and20**, **Veritasium**, and **Economics Explained** transcript patterns + SA script case study.  
> **Companion:** [`writing-style-guide.md`](writing-style-guide.md) · [`edit-patterns-from-sa-script.md`](edit-patterns-from-sa-script.md)

---

## Core rule

**Each section = one job. Each stat = one spoken landing. After that, point — don't re-read.**

| Creator | Speak | Show (card/B-roll only) |
|---|---|---|
| **2and20** | Mechanism, named people, one chart *insight* | Stats on chart; later say "revolving door" not "1,700 officials" again |
| **Veritasium** | Wrong belief → debunk once → build correct model | Re-arguing the misconception every section |
| **Economics Explained** | One puzzle per section; explicit roadmap | Stat piles that recap prior sections |

---

## Five-question test (every paragraph)

| # | Question | If "no" → |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **JOB** — Does this answer a question the last section didn't? | Cut or merge sections |
| 2 | **NEW** — Is this a new mechanism, or the same stat in new words? | Cut |
| 3 | **PROOF** — Is this the *first* time the audience needs this stat spoken? | Move to card only |
| 4 | **POINTER** — Can I say "look back at those charts" / "Yeah, those have not moved"? | Use callback, not re-read |
| 5 | **EARN** — Does this need VO, or can the card carry it with one TH line? | Card + 30s TH |

---

## Section job map (timeline documentaries)

| Section | Question it answers | One mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Is the headline explanation wrong? | Human moment + chart proof |
| S1 | What was decided at the fork? | Political vs economic transition |
| S2 | What did that produce on the ground? | Single institutional mechanism (e.g. cadre deployment) |
| S3 | What did scandal add? | Acceleration, not origin |
| S4 | What happened when they tried the obvious fix? | Partial recovery; structural unchanged |
| S5 | What would actually need to change? | **One** reframe + **one** lever block — not a re-trial of S1–4 |

## Stat ledger (SA script reference)

| Stat / idea | Speak fully (once) | Callback allowed | Don't speak again |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inequality flat 30 years | Hook chart (~1 sentence) | "those charts" / split card | "most unequal on earth" |
| Youth unemployment | Hook chart + S4 dials (one line) | Icon grid | "six in ten" in S5 + close |
| Land 7%/73% | S1 bars | — | Checklist re-list in S3/S5 |
| Land reform 11% | S4 | Card labels in S5 | Spoken % in S5 reframe |
| Cadre deployment | S2 full block | S3 one line | Full re-explain in S5 |
| Corruption ≠ whole story | Hook flip | "acceleration not origin" | Re-argue in S5 |

**Search-and-destroy on second draft:** `most unequal`, `highest on earth`, `highest unemployment`, repeated `six in ten`, repeated `eleven percent`, `barely moved` (max 2× total).

## Speak vs show matrix

| Always speak | Card / B-roll only | Usually waste |
|---|---|---|
| Human cost (queues, food looting) | Policy list items (procurement, JSE mechanics) | Stat recap paragraphs |
| Causal chain ("when loyalty > skill, institutions stop working") | Numbers already on screen | "That matters" / "this matters" |
| Oral pivots (*If only…*, *Yeah, those…*) | Public Protector / coalition detail | Five alternate closing beats |
| TH thesis + 3-bullet levers | Five "why slow" labels | NGO briefing-deck VO |
| Mandela quote + one interpretation | Repeated superlatives | Essay bridges before charts |

---

## Close discipline (2and20 pattern)

After the intellectual payoff, allow **60–90 seconds of literary escalation** over the defining image (Sandton–Alexandra, queue return, Mandela quote). This is cinema — full sentences, building beats, one global line at the end.

**Structure:** queue bookend → two revolutions → quote → interpretation → aerial hold → escalating hope (3–4 beats max) → subscribe

**Still cut from the close:** stat re-reads (*six in ten*, *eleven percent*, *most unequal*) — prove those earlier; inspire without numbers here.

Not: policy VO, corruption re-trial, or five redundant thesis lines saying the same thing.

---

## TH placement (~5–6 min in 11–13 min video)

| TH beat | When |
|---|---|
| Hook | Wrong-assumption flip + show title |
| S5 | Why hard (short) + 3 levers (short) |
| Sign-off | Subscribe |

Everything else = VO over timeline, data cards, B-roll.

---

## Pre-film / pre-Word checklist

- [ ] Section headers answer a **question**, not a topic label only  
- [ ] Each `[SHOW:]` card introduces at most **one** new spoken stat  
- [ ] Read aloud: highlight superlatives — second same-colour highlight = cut  
- [ ] Section 5 does not re-prosecute corruption or re-read hook stats  
- [ ] Close: count beats after Mandela — **max 2** before subscribe  
- [ ] TH includes *"This is [Title]"* in hook
