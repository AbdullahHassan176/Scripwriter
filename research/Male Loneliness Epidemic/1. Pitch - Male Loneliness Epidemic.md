# Video Concept:

Exploring the Male Loneliness epidemic and the impact of gaming on this

# Title:

Title Options:

# TLDR:

**What is the key takeaway from this story?**

Gaming has the potential to lower the barrier to vulnerability for men. By putting men in an environment with a shared goal (which creates a comfortable low-pressure buffer) and allowing for the possibility of anonymity, men are more likely to open up about real life struggles

When played in moderation, games successfully reduce levels of loneliness for low-risk gamers, allowing for the shoulder-to-shoulder bonding

But if it is a primary coping mechanism, it can create the illusion of socialising, which might result in displacing real world relationships with virtual relationships. This results in a social atrophy of their real world skills, resulting in higher social anxiety when forced to interact offline, causing a retreat back to the screen 

**What is game changing/assumption breaking about this story?**

We all thought that gaming is a waste of time, now it might be the only solution to the male loneliness problem
